# Grab-and-Go and Soup

The failure mode of this diet is being hungry with nothing compliant nearby. These fix it.

## Cupboard staples
- Tinned black beans, pinto beans, lentils (rinse, they are ready to eat)
- Tinned sardines, mackerel, tuna
- Pre-cooked lentil or bean pouches
- Frozen spinach and mixed vegetables

## Fridge staples
- Hard-boiled eggs, made six at a time
- Rotisserie chicken, pulled apart on the day you buy it
- Sauerkraut or kimchi

## Soup
A pot of bean-and-vegetable soup made once covers several meals and is the easiest way to
hit legumes without cooking daily. Log it with the *soup used* tag so a week of soup shows
up honestly in adherence rather than reading as skipped meals.

- Base: stock, onion, garlic, tinned tomatoes
- Bulk: lentils or mixed beans
- Protein: shredded chicken or beef mince
- Greens: spinach stirred in at the end
