# Sleep Reference

## Room temperature
67–70°F (19–21°C) is the tested range. As low as 65°F (18°C) works if you wear socks —
warm extremities let the body shed core heat, which is what triggers sleep onset.

## Darkness
Properly dark. Cover or remove LED indicators; blackout the window if street light gets in.

## Screens before bed
No screens in the last stretch before sleep. This is the check most often quietly skipped.

## Wine — timing, not just count
More than two glasses within four hours of bed cuts deep sleep by 20–50%. Two glasses
finished early in the evening is a different thing from two glasses at 11pm, which is why
the checklist treats this as one combined check rather than a count.

## Cold exposure before bed
An optional cold or cool bath before bed. Logged in the Cold Exposure pillar as the
*pre-bed bath* type, which is what this checklist item links to.

## Consistent wake time
The same wake time every day, including weekends, does more for sleep quality than a
consistent bedtime does.
