# Slow-Carb Rules

**Rule 1 — Avoid "white" starchy carbohydrates.**
No bread, rice (see Hybrid mode), cereal, potato, pasta, tortillas or fried food with
breading. If it is white, or was white before colouring, skip it.

**Rule 2 — Eat the same few meals over and over.**
Build each meal from three groups. Mix and match, but keep the list short — decision
fatigue is what breaks the diet, not hunger.

- **Protein:** eggs, chicken breast/thigh, beef, fish, pork
- **Legumes:** lentils, black beans, pinto beans, red beans, soy
- **Vegetables:** spinach, mixed veg, sauerkraut/kimchi, asparagus, peas, green beans

**Rule 3 — Don't drink calories.**
Water, unsweetened tea, coffee (limited cream), no more than 2 glasses of dry red wine
per night. No milk, soft drinks, or fruit juice.

**Rule 4 — Don't eat fruit.**
Exceptions: tomato and avocado, both in moderation.

**Rule 5 — Take one day off per week.**
One scheduled cheat day. Eat what you want. See *Damage Control* for the tactics that
blunt the damage without turning the day into a chore.

## Practical notes

- Eat within one hour of waking, aiming for 30g+ of protein at breakfast.
- Four meals a day is typical: ~8am, 12pm, 4pm(ish), 7:30pm.
- Legumes are what keep the meals filling — don't quietly drop them.
- Domino's-style "one bite won't hurt" foods are the ones to keep out of the house.
