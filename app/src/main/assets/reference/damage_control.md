# Damage Control (cheat day)

These are the non-supplement tactics for blunting a cheat day. They are **optional taps,
not requirements** — the cheat day is part of the plan, and the point here is to make it
cost less, not to make it stressful.

## 1. Protein and fibre first
Make the first meal of the day high in protein and fibre (eggs and lentils, for example)
before anything indulgent. It blunts appetite and flattens the insulin response for what
follows.

## 2. Citrus before the big meal
Grapefruit, lemon or lime juice shortly before the indulgent meal.

## 3. Move before the meal
60–90 seconds of bodyweight movement immediately before eating — air squats, wall presses.
Enough to recruit muscle, not enough to be a workout.

## 4. Move again after
The same 60–90 seconds roughly 90 minutes after the big meal.

## 5. Walk afterwards
A short walk after eating. Anything from 10 minutes up.

None of this needs to be complete to count. Two of five is better than none, and the log
exists to show what actually happened, not to grade the day.
