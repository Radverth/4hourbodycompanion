# Current Meal Plan

A short rotation. The point is repetition, not variety.

- 3 eggs scrambled + black beans + spinach
- Or: leftover dinner (this is normal and fine)

## Lunch
- Chicken thigh + lentils + mixed vegetables
- Or: beef mince + pinto beans + green beans

- Training day (Hybrid mode only): add rice

## Grab-and-go
- Tinned fish + pre-cooked lentil pouch
- Rotisserie chicken + tinned black beans
- Two hard-boiled eggs + handful of macadamias
