# Sauces and Seasoning

Flavour is what makes a repeated meal survivable. These add taste without breaking rules.

## Safe by default
- Salt, pepper, garlic, chilli flakes, cumin, smoked paprika
- Hot sauce (check for sugar — many are fine)
- Mustard, vinegar, lemon/lime juice
- Olive oil, butter, ghee
- Salsa (no added sugar), guacamole
- Soy sauce / tamari, fish sauce

## Check the label
- Ketchup, BBQ sauce, sweet chilli — usually sugar-loaded, treat as cheat-day items
- "Light" dressings — frequently sugar in place of fat
- Teriyaki, hoisin, oyster sauce — sugar

## Rule of thumb
If the first three ingredients include sugar, glucose syrup, or anything ending in "-ose",
it belongs on the cheat day, not on a Tuesday.
