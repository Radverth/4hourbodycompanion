# Hybrid Mode Rules

Hybrid mode is Slow-Carb with one deliberate change: **rice is allowed on training days**,
around the training window, to support strength work.

## What changes

- **Training days:** white or brown rice may be included with the post-training meal.
  The rice tag only appears in the meal log on a training day for exactly this reason.
- **Rest days:** identical to strict Slow-Carb. No rice.

## What does not change

- No liquid calories, no fruit (tomato and avocado excepted), no other white carbs.
- Protein + legume + vegetable is still the shape of every meal.
- One cheat day per week still applies.

Track which mode a day was logged under so adherence numbers stay honest — a rice meal on
a rest day is a miss in either mode.
